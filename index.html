Privacy Policy

Ladybird Dolls Dressing Ladybug Style
Developer: GameArtForKids
Android package: com.GameArtForKids.LadybirdDollsLadybugStyle
Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how this dress-up game and its service providers handle information. Contact us at gameartstudioteam@gmail.com with privacy or support questions.

Game progress stored on your device

Coins, purchases made with virtual game coins, outfit selections, tutorial completion and game preferences are stored locally on your device. Virtual game coins are in-game rewards, not a financial service. The game does not require you to create an account. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes local progress; device or platform backup settings may affect copies of that data.

Advertising and information sent to Google

The game uses Google's AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. These SDKs can collect and share information with Google, including an IP address (which may indicate an approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or other identifiers such as an app set ID. Google uses this information to deliver and measure advertising, operate its services, and prevent fraud and abuse.

Removing access to the Android advertising ID does not mean that the advertising SDK collects no information. The updated game disables access to that advertising ID and publisher first-party personalization features. Other advertising SDK information described above can still be processed. Data sent by Google's advertising SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Children and advertising choices

This game can be used by children. We configure advertising requests for child-directed treatment and under-age-of-consent handling. Updated releases request general-audience rated, non-personalized advertising, without profiling a child's age. Rewarded advertising is optional: a player chooses to watch it for a stated in-game reward. We do not ask children to provide their name, phone number, precise location or an account. We do not knowingly use the game to request personal information from children.

Where Google requires a consent or privacy-options message, its User Messaging Platform handles that process. Availability depends on age treatment, region and Google's configuration. Advertising data may still be processed for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention even when personalized advertising is disabled.

Analytics and earlier versions

Updated releases disable automatic Firebase Analytics collection, Firebase gameplay-event logging, Unity Analytics and Unity exception capture. This does not disable Google's advertising measurement and diagnostics. Earlier published versions may use Firebase Analytics and Unity analytics or diagnostics, which can process app interactions, performance information and identifiers. This policy also covers those earlier versions until they are replaced.

Information you send to support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and resolve the reported issue. Please do not send unnecessary personal information, especially information about a child.

Providers, retention and deletion

Google processes advertising and other SDK data under its own privacy terms. Provider retention periods and deletion options depend on the service and its legal obligations. We do not promise that uninstalling the game deletes information already processed by Google. Local game data remains until it is removed from the device. Support messages are kept as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations.

You can clear the game's local storage through Android settings and manage Google account and advertising privacy controls through Google's services. To request access to or deletion of information you sent directly to us, contact the support email above. We will handle requests subject to applicable law and any necessary identity verification. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child's information.
